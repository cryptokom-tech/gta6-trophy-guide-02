Roadmap to the platinum

Five stages in the order to play them, built only from the official trophy descriptions. Stage 1 is the story; the rest is best done after the credits, when the whole map is open. Each stage lists its trophies and its own progress.

Missable trophies

Every trophy tied to a specific story mission or moment, or to a one-time condition. Earn these on your playthrough — do not leave them for after the credits.

Our rule: a trophy is flagged missable when its official description ties it to a specific story mission, a chapter ending, a stranger strand's final mission, or a one-time condition such as a partner being with you. Mission replay is not verified for this game, so the flags are cautious on purpose — several of these may turn out to be replayable, and this page will say so when it is confirmed. Everything not listed here can be earned after the story.

Settings & data

Your progress lives in this browser's local storage — nothing leaves your machine. Export it to keep a backup, import it to restore one.

Spoilers

Story trophies are hidden by default: their names and descriptions read “Hidden story trophy — earn it to reveal” until you earn them. Earned trophies always show their real name.

Save data

Export downloads a JSON file with your earned trophies, counters and notes. Import reads one back. Reset clears everything and starts again at 0/55.

About this guide

Names, descriptions and grades are PlayStation's official list, as Exophase and PSNProfiles publish it. PSN counts (250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges, …) are from PSNProfiles; the 28 hidden flags follow PSN. Every guide is written in our own words from the official description — nothing was copied from another guide, and no location or step is claimed that the description does not pin down.

Heads up: PowerPyx reports that turning on a cheat disables trophies — play clean for the platinum. And there are no online trophies: all 55 are single-player.
